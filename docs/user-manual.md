# Teachfolio user manual

This manual explains how to use Teachfolio in a browser. The pages and actions available to you depend on your active role and institution. If a menu item described here is not visible, ask your institution administrator to check your account assignment.

## Before you begin

Teachfolio is a browser workspace for teaching-practice portfolios and review. You need an internet connection and an account invited to the correct institution. Creating an account alone does not grant access; an administrator must assign your role. Use a current browser and keep your sign-in details private.

The application is under active development. Follow your institution's policies for portfolio evidence, learner information, consent, and record retention. Do not enter identifiable learner information unless your institution has authorized it.

## Sign in and activate your account

1. Open the Teachfolio address supplied by your institution.
2. If you have an invitation, open its link and use the same email address to which it was sent. Create your account or sign in, then complete the invitation activation steps.
3. If email confirmation is requested, open the confirmation message and follow its link. Return to Teachfolio and sign in.
4. If you already have an activated account, choose **Sign in** and enter your email and password.
5. If you cannot remember the password, choose the password recovery option on the sign-in screen, request a reset email, and follow the link. Teachfolio staff cannot see your password.

Invitations are single-use and expire. If a link has expired, already been used, or was sent to the wrong address, ask the inviting administrator for a new invitation. An unverified, pending, or suspended account cannot open protected workspace pages.

## Find your way around

After signing in, the left navigation shows the pages enabled for your role. Select **Overview** to see the workspace landing page. Use the menu button on a narrow screen to open or close navigation. The profile menu provides sign-out; **Settings** contains your profile and personal preferences.

Common pages include **My placement**, **My portfolio**, **Schemes of work**, **Lesson plans**, **Reflective journal**, **Teaching evidence**, **Submissions**, **Review queue**, **Feedback & assessments**, **Reports**, **Placements**, **Portfolio requirements**, **User activation**, **Institutions**, and **Platform users**. Not every role sees every page.

Use the page's search, filters, and sort controls when available. Open a portfolio record to see its details, submission version, evidence files you are authorized to access, and review history.

## Student teacher: build and submit a portfolio

### Check your placement

Open **My placement** to check the school and placement information assigned to you. If details are missing or incorrect, contact your coordinator or institution administrator; students do not edit placement assignments.

### Create a portfolio item

1. Open **My portfolio** or the specific section such as **Schemes of work**, **Lesson plans**, **Reflective journal**, or **Teaching evidence**.
2. Select **Add portfolio item** (or the corresponding add action) and choose the item type.
3. Enter the requested title and teaching-practice details. Complete the fields relevant to that item; available fields vary by type.
4. Choose **Save draft** to store work you intend to continue later. For evidence, **Save metadata only** records its description without transferring a file.
5. Reopen the record from your portfolio to continue editing or check that it was saved.

Save drafts regularly. A draft is not submitted for review. The app may show validation errors beside required or incorrectly formatted fields; correct them and save again.

### Add an evidence file

1. Open or create a teaching evidence item and complete its metadata.
2. Confirm the required consent statement when applicable.
3. Select an allowed file (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JPEG, PNG, or MP4; maximum 25 MiB by default).
4. Choose **Save metadata and upload** and keep the dialog open until upload completion is confirmed.
5. Reopen the record and check that the file appears.

An evidence description can be saved without a file. Uploading is a separate action. If the upload fails, check file type and size, connection, and consent, then retry. Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

### Submit work

1. Review the item for completeness and accuracy. Follow the portfolio requirements shown by your institution.
2. Open the item and choose **Save and submit**.
3. Confirm the submission action if prompted. The submitted version is recorded as a snapshot for reviewers.
4. Open **Submissions** or the item detail to check its status and later feedback.

After submission, the reviewer assesses that submitted version. You cannot silently change the version under review. If a reviewer requests changes, edit the item and submit the revision; Teachfolio keeps version and review history. Contact your reviewer if you need clarification before resubmitting.

## Supervisor and lecturer: review work

1. Open **Review queue**. It lists submitted work for student teachers assigned to you through a placement.
2. Use the status or search controls to find work. Open the submitted-version details and read the record and its authorized evidence.
3. Select **Start review** where available. The version being reviewed is read-only and remains the review target even if the student later creates a revision.
4. Complete the rubric scores and criterion comments, then add an overall decision and feedback.
5. Submit the review and verify that it appears in the record's review history.

Review only students assigned to you, and do not review your own work. If an expected student or submission is missing, ask the coordinator to check the placement and reviewer links. A review decision applies to the version shown in the review screen.

## Coordinator: manage placements and requirements

### Manage a placement

1. Open **Placements**.
2. Find the student or placement, or use the available filters.
3. Create or update the placement details using the fields presented.
4. Link the student teacher and the appropriate supervisor and/or lecturer, then save.
5. Reopen the placement to confirm the saved assignment. The linked reviewers use these assignments to receive work in their review queues.

Placement actions are limited to your institution. Correct an assignment promptly if a reviewer or student changes. Institution administrators may also manage placements.

### Manage portfolio requirements and reports

Open **Portfolio requirements** to review or update the institution's expected portfolio work. Check the requirement descriptions before saving changes so students understand what to prepare. Open **Reports** to review institution-scoped portfolio information and use an available CSV export when needed. Treat exported data as confidential and store it according to institutional policy.

## Institution administrator: manage your institution

### Invite a user

1. Open **User activation**.
2. Enter the person's email address and select the appropriate institution role offered by the form.
3. Send the invitation and tell the person to use that same email address when signing up or signing in.
4. Check the pending invitation/activation status. If it expires or is addressed incorrectly, send a replacement invitation.

Use the least access needed for the person's duties. Invitations grant institution access only after the recipient verifies their email and redeems the invitation. Institution administrators cannot grant platform administrator access. An institution must retain at least one active institution administrator.

### Review administration and audit activity

Use **Placements** and **Portfolio requirements** for the workflows described above. Use **Reports** for institution-scoped summaries/exports. Open **Audit log** to inspect recorded administrative activity. You can manage users and assignments only within your institution; platform-suspended or unverified users cannot be reactivated from institution administration.

## Platform administrator: platform operations

Platform administration is restricted to trusted, provisioned platform administrators.

- **Institutions:** create or edit institution records and manage their active, paused, or archived state. Check the institution details before saving.
- **Platform users:** search the directory, inspect account assignments and activity, and use the available account-management actions. Account suspension, recovery, verification, and deletion affect access or identity; review the target account carefully before acting. Deletion anonymizes the profile and retains linked academic/audit records.
- **Audit log:** inspect and export platform-scoped administrative records where available.
- **Settings:** update platform invitation expiry and evidence upload cap within the limits shown.

The dashboard does not grant or remove platform administrator privileges. Those changes require the trusted server-side provisioning process. Follow the separate [system guide](system-guide.md) and [staging deployment guide](staging-deployment.md) for operational and deployment procedures.

## Profile, notifications, and password

Open **Settings**:

- Change your **Display name**, then select **Save profile**.
- Turn **Email workflow notifications** on or off. This preference controls workflow emails; in-app notifications are separate.
- Under **Password and security**, request a password reset link. Complete the change through the email from the authentication provider.

Email, role, and institution are shown as account context and are not self-editable there. Contact an administrator about incorrect identity or role details. Sign out on shared devices.

## Statuses and review history

Portfolio status labels indicate where work is in its workflow. A **draft** is saved for continued editing; **submitted** work has been sent for review; a request for changes means the student should revise and resubmit; a reviewed/accepted result records a decision for the version assessed. Exact status labels and available actions can differ by record type and role. Open the item and read the latest reviewer feedback rather than relying only on a dashboard count.

Review history is versioned. Each decision and its comments belong to the submitted version the reviewer saw. A revision creates another submission version and does not erase the earlier decision.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in says access is unavailable | Confirm your email is verified and that you accepted the invitation. Ask the administrator to check that your assignment is active and that you are not suspended. |
| Invitation link does not work | Use the invited email address. Ask the inviter to check expiry or issue a new invitation. |
| Password reset email does not arrive | Check spam/junk and confirm the address. If it still does not arrive, contact the administrator. |
| A menu/page is missing | Your active role may not include it. Ask the administrator to verify your assignment. |
| Placement or review queue is empty | Ask the coordinator to confirm your student/reviewer placement links and that work has been submitted. |
| Save or upload fails | Check required fields, connection, file type/size, and consent. Keep the upload dialog open until it reports completion, then retry. |
| A page reports that the service is unavailable | Refresh once and try again. If the problem persists, report the page, time, and action to your administrator; do not send passwords, reset links, or evidence files in the report. |

## Privacy and safe use

- Use only an account assigned to you; do not share credentials or invitation/reset links.
- Upload only evidence approved by your institution and obtain required school/guardian consent before adding identifiable learner media.
- Evidence files are private and access is checked by role, ownership, institution, and placement assignment. Do not copy sensitive files into public links or unauthorized storage.
- Treat reports and CSV exports as confidential. Share them only with authorized people and remove local copies when institutional policy requires it.
- Contact your institution administrator promptly about mistaken access, a misdirected invitation, or suspected account exposure.

For role boundaries and technical limitations, see [roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) and the [system guide](system-guide.md).
